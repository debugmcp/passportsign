Bind your GitHub account to a passport-verified human

Four steps, all in this tab: prove control of your GitHub account with a gist, scan your passport with the ZKPassport app, and the binding is published to the public Sigstore Rekor transparency log. This page holds no server-side state — your passport data never leaves your phone, and the proof reveals nothing but personhood (plus country, only if you choose).

Read before binding — these are permanent:
  • If you disclose your country, that disclosure is in the public log forever.
  • Multiple accounts bound with the same passport are visibly linked, forever. Revocation marks a binding inactive but the log entry remains.
  • The gist URL and a hash of its content are captured in the log; deleting the gist later doesn't remove them.
  • Anyone with brief access to your passport can revoke your binding (you can re-bind to recover).
